SCIENTIFIC ASSUMPTIONS AND LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
1. This utility performs an INSTANTANEOUS RAAN-to-LTAN conversion
   at the specified epoch. It does NOT propagate the orbit.
2. It does NOT calculate J2 nodal precession or future LTAN drift.
3. It does NOT calculate launch windows.
4. It does NOT calculate launch azimuth or launch vehicle injection
   parameters.
5. It does NOT calculate ground-station visibility or communication
   windows.
6. The BITS Goa ground station does NOT affect the RAAN-to-LTAN
   conversion.
7. The launch site and launch provider do NOT affect the conversion
   once the orbit and epoch are specified.
8. Orbit altitude and inclination are recorded for mission
   traceability but are NOT direct inputs to this instantaneous
   conversion.
9. The assumed epoch is the reference epoch of the supplied RAAN
   values. It is NOT necessarily the actual launch or orbital
   insertion epoch.
10. If the actual orbital insertion time is unknown, the default
    epoch must be treated as PROVISIONAL.
11. The current 97.6° inclination is retained as an ANTARA working
    assumption. The script does NOT silently modify it or claim
    that it guarantees a perfectly Sun-synchronous orbit.
12. The result is dependent on the adopted solar-coordinate
    convention. Mean LTAN and apparent solar time are NOT
    interchangeable.
13. The astronomical model (mean-Sun, low-precision) should be
    validated against a trusted ephemeris (e.g., JPL DE430, SPICE)
    before using results for final PDR/CDR mission decisions.
14. CONVERSION EQUATION: LTAN = [12 + (Ω - α☉)/15] mod 24
    where Ω = RAAN (deg), α☉ = Solar RA (deg), LTAN in hours.
15. SOLAR MODEL: Mean-Sun model per Meeus 'Astronomical Algorithms'
    (Ch. 25). Uses mean longitude, mean anomaly, equation of
    center, and mean obliquity. Expected accuracy ~0.01 deg.
